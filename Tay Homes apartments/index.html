How do I book an apartment?

Send your preferred dates and guest details using the enquiry form. Our team will confirm availability and guide you through the next steps.

Are families welcome?

Yes. Tay Homes welcomes families. Please mention your group size when you enquire so we can confirm the best apartment arrangement.

Is Wi-Fi available?

High-speed Wi-Fi is among the listed amenities. Get in touch if you have specific connectivity requirements.

Is parking available?

Secure parking is available at the property.

Do you offer long stays?

Ask us about extended stays, availability, and rates for the dates you have in mind.

What amenities are included?

Facilities include Wi-Fi, a shared kitchen, meeting hall, swimming pool, outdoor relaxation area, gym equipment, secure parking, and air conditioning. Please confirm details when booking.
